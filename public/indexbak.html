Why Tupac Matters Here

A quick infographic-style overview and the connection to the film’s message.

Rapper Poet Actor

Who he was

Tupac Shakur (1971–1996) was an American rapper and actor known for music that spoke about poverty, racism, police violence, and survival. He became one of the most influential voices in hip-hop because he turned social pain into lyrics people could not ignore.

Core ideas in his work

  • Speaking honestly about injustice and trauma
  • Community pride alongside criticism of systems
  • Anger as a reaction to being unheard
  • Hope + responsibility: “what we pass on” matters

Connection to the film

The film’s message is similar: when pain is ignored, it spreads. Starr’s story shows how one event is never “only one event”—it affects families, schools, communities, and what people believe is normal.
